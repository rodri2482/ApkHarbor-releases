ApkHarbor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

ApkHarbor is a free Android catalog of games and apps. This policy explains what the app stores, why, who else is involved, and how to delete it. If you have a question, email x86go.support@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google or with an email and password, our sign-in service keeps your email address, display name and, for Google sign-in, your profile picture.
  • What you post. Reviews and star ratings (with the display name you choose and, if you add it, a short device description), whether a download link worked for you, issue reports, game requests and your votes on them.
  • Your profile. Your username, the picture, banner and colour you choose, and when your account was made.
  • Pictures you upload. A picture or banner you upload from your gallery goes to our image host. Location and camera details are removed from it first. An admin looks at it before anyone else can see it, and a rejected one is deleted.
  • Your library. The games you save or follow, so the list follows you to another phone and so you can be told when a followed game is updated.
  • Downloads you open. A private record of which downloads your account opened, so only people who opened a game's download can rate or review it.
  • Notifications, if you turn them on. A code that lets our server send notifications to this phone, linked to your account until you turn them off or sign out.
  • On your phone only. Your theme, your link history, your search history, the games you opened (used for recommendations), downloaded files and their progress, games installed with ApkHarbor, notification settings and some saved posts. We never receive these.

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number or personal files. ApkHarbor has no ads, no advertising ID and no analytics or tracking tools.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your library, reviews and requests tied to your account.
  • To show reviews, ratings and link checks to other people, so they can judge a post.
  • To tell you when a game you follow has a new version or your request changes.
  • To review reports and keep the catalog free of spam and broken links.
  • To fix crashes. If the app crashes, it sends a short report the next time it opens: a random report number, the error, the time, the app and Android versions, and your phone's brand and model. It contains nothing about your account, and it is deleted after 90 days.

Recommendations

"Recommended for you" uses the games you opened, saved and downloaded. For signed-in accounts, their names, details and how long ago you used them go through our server to an AI service, which picks from the catalog. Your name, email address and account number are not sent, and our server does not keep the list.

Who else is involved

  • Cloud hosting. Accounts and online data are kept by cloud service providers that store them for us under their own security and privacy commitments, and only process them to run the app.
  • Videos. When you tap a trailer, it plays from the video platform that hosts it, which can see that you watched it.
  • Download sites. ApkHarbor does not host game or app files. They come from other websites, whether you download inside the app or a button opens the site. Those sites have their own privacy policies and can see your connection.
  • Admin tools. Posts are written by the ApkHarbor admins, who use outside services to host pictures and to help write descriptions and overviews. None of your personal data is sent to these services.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Who can see what

Your display name, reviews and ratings are public inside the app. Your username is public too. Anyone signed in who taps your review can also see your profile: its approved picture and banner, colour, the date you joined, how many reviews you've written, your average stars and your two newest reviews. If you pick a saved game as your banner, that one game shows. Your email address, the rest of your saved and followed lists, download history, link checks, reports and votes are not shown to other users. If someone reports your profile, the admins see the report and your display name. ApkHarbor admins can read reports and game requests to handle them.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. To delete it, open You in the app and choose Delete account. This removes your account, your profile, the pictures you uploaded, your username (which someone else may then choose), your saved and followed lists, your reviews and ratings, link checks, request votes and the game requests you created, and clears the app's data on your phone. Issue reports may be kept so admins can finish handling them; email us if you want them removed too. Files you downloaded stay in your phone's Download/ApkHarbor folder until you delete them. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and access rules limit each person to their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

ApkHarbor is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing x86go.support@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, say so in the app's What's New notes.
