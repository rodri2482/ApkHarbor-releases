ApkHarbor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

ApkHarbor is a free Android catalog of games and apps. This policy explains what the app stores, why, who else is involved, and how to delete it. If you have a question, email x86go.support@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google or with an email and password, our sign-in service keeps your email address, display name and, for Google sign-in, your profile picture.
  • What you post. Reviews and star ratings (with the display name you choose and, if you add it, a short device description), whether a download link worked for you, issue reports, game requests and your votes on them.
  • Your library. The games you save or follow, so the list follows you to another phone and so you can be told when a followed game is updated.
  • On your phone only. Your theme, your link history, notification settings, and some cached posts. These never leave the device.

We do not collect your location, contacts, phone number, or files. ApkHarbor has no ads, no advertising ID and no analytics or tracking tools.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your library, reviews and requests tied to your account.
  • To show reviews, ratings and link checks to other people, so they can judge a post.
  • To tell you, with a phone notification, when a game you follow is updated or your request changes. The app checks for this itself about every 6 hours; no message service is involved.
  • To review reports and keep the catalog free of spam and broken links.

Who else is involved

  • Cloud hosting. Accounts and online data are kept by a cloud service provider that stores them for us under its own security and privacy commitments, and only processes them to run the app.
  • Videos. When you tap a trailer, it plays from the video platform that hosts it, which can see that you watched it.
  • Download sites. ApkHarbor does not host game or app files. Download buttons open other websites, which have their own privacy policies.
  • Admin tools. Posts are written by the ApkHarbor admins, who use outside services to host pictures and to help write descriptions and overviews from a post's own text. None of your personal data is sent to these services.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Who can see what

Your display name, reviews and ratings are public inside the app. Your email address, saved and followed lists, link checks, reports and votes are not shown to other users. ApkHarbor admins can read reports and game requests to handle them.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. To delete it, open You in the app and choose Delete account. This removes your account, your saved and followed lists, your reviews and ratings, link checks, request votes and the game requests you created, and clears the app's data on your phone. Issue reports may be kept so admins can finish handling them; email us if you want them removed too. If you cannot open the app, email us from the address on your account and we will delete it for you.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections, and access rules limit each person to their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

Children

ApkHarbor is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how we use it, by emailing x86go.support@gmail.com. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, say so in the app's What's new notes.
